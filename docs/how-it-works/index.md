# How it works

The interesting parts of Blattwerk are not the screens but the rules behind them. Each page explains one piece,
how it is built, and why it is built that way.

- **[The to-do engine](todo-engine.md)** – tasks and occurrences, intervals that count from the day you did it,
  generations instead of overwrites, the soil feedback loop.
- **[German law in code](legal.md)** – the § 39 bird-protection period, tree bylaws, why rules are not prompts.
- **[Season and weather](season-weather.md)** – phenology, MOSMIX, sensors, alert rules with hysteresis and
  per-plant hardiness.
- **[The AI pipeline](ai.md)** – from facts to prompt to schema to sanitised plan; budgets, limits, evaluation.
- **[Chat and MCP](chat.md)** – tools instead of prompt stuffing, a read-only database role, proposals.
- **[Plant doctor and photos](plant-doctor.md)** – upload, metadata stripping, images to Claude, diagnoses.
- **[Indoor plants](indoor.md)** – rooms and windows, watering only inside, seasonal intervals.
